MooHunt

Blend in with the herd and try to act natural! Every now and then you have to make your animal's sound into the mic – if you stay quiet for too long, your animal starts spinning and gives you away. As the hunter, watch the herd closely and catch your friends, but be careful not to hit the real animals!

  • 🎲 Everyone gets a different farm animal each round.
  • 🎙 Grab a pair of headphones so your mic doesn't echo.
  • 🔊 Hold down M anytime to talk or make noises live.
  • 💩 Press P as an animal to drop a poop and sneak +1 point!
